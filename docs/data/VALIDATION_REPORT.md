## Reconciliations
- Weight: SUM(material kg) = batch incoming x 1000 within 0.1% (exact integer-kg scaling).
- Output: completed batches outputs = incoming; open batches outputs + backlog = incoming (<=0.5%).
- Route: material route weights = RecoveryOutputs per batch (<=0.5 t).
- Cost: batch Total Recovery Cost = SUM(CostLedger core 5 categories).
- Value: gross_value_aud = weight_t x unit_value_aud_per_t on every row.
- Dates: source <= arrival <= events; inspection open <= close; output >= arrival; costs within lifecycle.
- Statuses: completed batches have no open inspections, unresolved materials or open events.

## Identified limitations
1. All values are synthetic prototype assumptions; no real company, benchmark or market standard implied.
2. No structural safety, chemical safety or certification claims.
3. Month-end backlog snapshots are generator-computed states from raw event/inspection timelines.
4. Monthly net recovery value attributes outputs and core costs to their event/cost-date months; short-period North Star values can be volatile.
5. Batch-level inspections (material_id = N/A) carry no weight in backlog / KPI-06 union.
6. North Star includes the economic effect of open/backlogged material; it is not accounting profit.
7. Comparison periods are limited to what 2025 data supports (e.g. December vs November); no 2024 records are fabricated.
8. Open batches are Provisional: their current values are cost incurred to date / value realised to date and are excluded from realised rankings and quadrant medians.
9. Inspection exposure rate is a lifecycle share; current inspection state is a separate snapshot metric.
